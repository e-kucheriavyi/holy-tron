Rules

  • Player can move left, right, up or down.
  • Player moves every tick.
  • Player leaves trace behind.
  • Colliding with any trace kills player.
  • Leaving boundaries of a map kills player.
  • Last alive player wins.

Network

  1. Connect to WebSocket (endpoint is below).
  2. Send your nickname (if phase is PENDING).
  3. Wait for game to start.
  4. Control your player by sending single character: "l" | "r" | "u" | "d" (see Direction in types below).
